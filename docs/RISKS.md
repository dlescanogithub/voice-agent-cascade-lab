# Risks

Owners are roles. "Lab maintainer" is the person who merges this repo. "Clinic operations" and "platform owner" exist only if a later pilot exists. Probability and impact are judgments for this lab, marked as such, not statistics from production.

| Risk | Probability | Impact | Mitigation | Owner |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A retry books the same slot twice | Low in this lab (unique index plus idempotency key) | High if it ever reached a real calendar | Invariant `double_booking`. R01–R05. Do not connect a system that cannot honor a key. | Platform owner for a pilot. Lab maintainer for this repo. |
| The agent states a dose or a diagnosis | Low while the guard and the cassette stay as tested | High | Invariant `clinical_advice`. Forced regression proves the gate sees it. | Clinic operations for the phrase list. Lab maintainer for the code. |
| Aggressive VAD cuts on noise and the caller hears a broken sentence | High if that profile is selected (SIMULATED false-cut rate 1.0, by construction, 2026-10-17) | Medium | Gate requires zero false cuts. Balanced is the default. | Platform owner chooses the profile. Lab maintainer keeps the gate. |
| Patient VAD waits through a long pause and the call feels slow | Medium by design (p95 e2e 1413.75 ms SIMULATED) | Low inside the lab budget, unknown on a real line | Report the three profiles side by side. Do not pick patient without a wall-clock measurement from `python -m voice_cascade live`. | Platform owner |
| Webhook hangs mid-call and the caller sits in silence | Medium once a real dependency exists. Not observable on the in-process transport. | High | Per-attempt timeout, capped retries, DLQ, escalation copy. Runbook section "webhook stuck". | Platform owner |
| Dead-letter queue grows and nobody replays it | Medium in a pilot, low in the lab (one scripted case) | Medium: missed bookings, no double book | `dlq-replay`, runbook "DLQ growing", alert on queue length (not built here). | Platform owner |
| Logs keep a synthetic or real phone number | Low after redaction tests | High if a real number ever entered the process | Redaction of phone keys and patterns. Invariant `pii`. Do not load a real extract. | Lab maintainer |
| Someone treats a SIMULATED p95 as a service level | High if the README box is skipped | High reputational | Honest-limits box. The Measured with real providers table stays empty until the owner pastes a live run. | Lab maintainer |
| The live availability check misses or over-flags a slot | Medium. `unsupported_availability_claim` is a heuristic, not a parser. On 2026-10-17 two fresh handwritten sets, each n=20 and separate from the unit tests, were scored separately. Set 1 caught 11 of 20 (55%, about ±22 points) and 0 of 20 clean sentences. Set 2, scored on the heuristic after the 0.1.6 patterns, caught 15 of 20 (75%) and 1 of 20 clean sentences (a medical fast read as a clock time). The lists differ, n is small, and the intervals overlap, so 75% is not an improvement over 55%. An earlier set, measured before the patterns in pull request 7, caught 14 of 20 claims and 1 of 18 clean sentences. Recall after the patterns in this release is not measured yet. False negatives and false positives are both expected. A negation, a cancellation policy, or a medical duration can hide a later offer in the same sentence. | Medium: a caller could hear a time that was not looked up, or a refusal could be replaced | Keep the check. Do not treat a green or red invariant as a review. A person still reads the transcript. | Lab maintainer |
| A token times price figure is quoted as the provider bill | Medium if `results.json` is skimmed | Medium reputational | `cost.estimated_usd` is the only tokens×price field (`cost_basis` `tokens_x_price, cached tokens priced separately`). `provider_reported_usd` is written only from integer `usage.cost_in_usd_ticks` (1 tick = 1e-10 USD). `usage.cost` and a pre-filled dollar field are ignored. The rollup does not trust a replayed `provider_reported_usd`. | Lab maintainer |
| Demo HMAC constant reused outside the laptop | Low if the docs are followed | High | `SECURITY.md`. Secret scan. Empty `.env.example`. | Whoever deploys |
| Cassette and script drift so the intent no longer matches the tool | Medium as phrases change | Medium | Outcome invariant, not a golden trajectory. Tests lock the balanced cohort. | Lab maintainer |
